# Code of Conduct

Talkr should be a welcoming place to learn, build, and disagree constructively.

## Be a good human

- Be respectful, patient, and curious.
- Critique ideas and code—not people.
- Welcome newcomers and make room for different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Assume good intent, while taking responsibility for your impact.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about anyone.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.

If something happens, report it privately to the project maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/alihassan-coder). Reports will be handled with care and shared only when necessary.

In short: make Talkr better, and leave the community better too.
